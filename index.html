Open-source, coming soon

Brahmandam

A PyTorch toolkit for training controllable world models from video game environments. The goal is to make existing games usable as causal, interactive, intervention-aware environments rather than just next-frame prediction datasets.

Built with Soumik Rakshit. Includes gameplay data capture, observation-input alignment, trajectory datasets, video-prediction models, latent dynamics models, action-conditioned simulators, and early sub-1B parameter world models already trained.

PyTorch World Models Causal representation learning Action-conditioned simulation
